About the Student Management System

Welcome to the Student Management System, a comprehensive and user-friendly platform designed to manage and organize student and staff data seamlessly.

The system offers the following core features:

Student Data Management

Manage student records like personal details, courses, and history.

Staff Data Management

Track staff info such as name, role, and department.

Search Functionality

Quickly search students or staff data.
