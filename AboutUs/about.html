About the Student Management System

Welcome to the Student Management System, a comprehensive and user-friendly platform designed to manage and organize student and staff data seamlessly. The system enables users to store, search, and update vital information with ease, ensuring smooth operation within educational institutions.

The system offers the following core features:

Student Data Management

Efficiently manage student records, including personal details, courses, and academic history. Add, edit, or delete data as needed.

Staff Data Management

Keep track of staff details such as name, role, department, and contact information, allowing for better coordination and management.

Search Functionality

Easily search for students or staff by their details, making information retrieval quick and hassle-free.
